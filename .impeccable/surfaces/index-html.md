# Surface: the board (index.html, the whole app)

Mode: Operate. A musician with an instrument in hand sets a tempo or tunes, the device 1-2 m away on a stand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tactus is a pedalboard. Each tool is one enamel stompbox with a black display window, one big knob, small selector knobs and chrome footswitches; the toolbox is the board they sit on. Refuses the category default: dark app panels, a round gauge with a needle, pill buttons, plus/minus steppers.

OWN-WORLD: Dark board (#17171a) with horizontal mounting rails. Pedals in powder-coated enamel: Tempo in vermilion (#b83a28), Tuner in bottle green (#2f6d5a), future pedals mustard (#e2b33c) and slate blue. Silkscreen ink cream (#f3ead8). Display windows are recessed black glass with warm white numerals and LEDs: red = status/accent, amber = beat/off-pitch, green only for in tune. Knurled black knobs with cream pointer, chicken-head selectors, chrome stomp switches with hex nut, side jacks. One family: Archivo variable; expanded caps for silkscreen, condensed for readings.

STORY: Open, the Tempo pedal is in front; stomp the switch, it clicks on the audio clock; turn the knob or tap the TAP switch; swipe (or look right on desktop) to the Tuner, stomp it, play a string, watch the LED bar centre and turn green.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: header strip with TACTUS wordmark, mini-pedal tabs, language switch. One pedal fills the rest: display window top third (BPM at reading size, tempo name, one LED per beat, tap an LED to change its accent), knob row middle (big TEMPO knob, BEATS / SUBDIV / VOICE / LEVEL selectors), two footswitches in the thumb zone at the bottom (TAP left, ON right with red LED). Desktop: both pedals side by side at full height on the board, cabled jack to jack.

FORM: Pedalboard, position 1 of my ordered list (my pick card); seed key ed9fe15e. Signature interaction: the knob (drag, wheel, arrows) and the stomp (switch depresses, LED lights on the audio clock). Motion grammar: physical, short, state-only; beat LEDs flash from audio-clock timestamps; tuner LED bar eases.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
